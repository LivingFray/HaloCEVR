Changes
1. SeatAnchor works in vehicle-local coordinates. During settling it follows
   the sampled head with a70ms filter. Lock requires >=0.75s since start,
   >=0.30s of per-frame motion<=1.2cm, and target residual<2cm. Once locked,
   small idle motion is ignored. Displacement>12cm reopens settling regardless
   of elapsed time, preventing a long/paused entry from permanently freezing.
   Vehicle/seat/unit changes and recenter reset calibration. Existing 25ms
   chassis smoothing, eye lift, direct HMD tracking and render restoration stay.
   A locked transition logs seat index and head residual in metres.
2. Chest offsets in metres, forward/left/up relative to HMD yaw:
   frag (0.06,-0.10,-0.24), plasma(0.06,+0.10,-0.24).
   Previous lateral offsets were +/-0.15 and height -0.32. Grab radius stays
   0.12, return0.17. Selection now minimizes distance across available grenade
   zones, rather than checking frag first. Existing nearer weapon/supply
   priority and occupied-hand restrictions are preserved.
3. GrenadeVelocity doubles tracked speed before the saved strength multiplier;
   maximum hand contribution is24m/s. Existing saved1.3 becomes effective2.6
   instead of changing a default that existing config files would override.
   Native velocity remains world units/tick: clamp(world/sec)/30+player/tick.
   Direction, zero-velocity drops, inventory/fuse and native release untouched.
   Log records measured hand speed and capped requested launch speed, excluding
   inherited player motion. It does not claim a measured range in metres.

- Actual production adapter tests simulate immediate and delayed climb-in for
  all three supported seats, including changing chassis yaw, settling, idle
  jitter, unsmoothed physical HMD lean, exit and frustum/camera restoration.
- Actual grab adapter covers both hands/types at overlapping centre zones.
- Velocity tests exercise actual metres/world/tick conversion, saved1.3, gentle
  motion, hard throws/cap, inherited player motion and stationary drop.
- Existing native-memory, hand pose, reload/audio, holster, recoil and input
  suite runs with undefined-behaviour checks.
- Native profile/ABI addresses and model-draw hook are unchanged. R1.23's
  recorded EXE verification is retained; the EXE fixture was not rerun here.
- No MSVC build, executable execution or headset/GPU test performed. Final
  camera placement/comfort and throw range remain in-game validation items.
