Native rasterizer_set_frustum_z at 0x518f40 updates the rasterizer frustum
(0x7c127c) and shader constants. The override at 0x50c9a0 sets projection
_13/_23=0, _33=-(f+n)/(f-n), _43=-2*f*n/(f-n). The first-person model paths
read near/far from 0x69c664/0x69c668: 0.01171875 and 1024, confirmed in the
binary. Examples: 0x519fac, 0x527110, 0x5337b5, 0x533f45. The prior custom
mesh pass used the unmodified world projection; resetting viewport MinZ/MaxZ
alone cannot correct incompatible projected depths. RenderProjection.h now
uses the native first-person override for the procedural body marker/pieces.
Native grenade previews, including grey empty previews, reuse the native
model path and its depth behavior. GreyMarkerDraw changes color/blend/fog,
not depth state. Actual GPU occlusion remains to be tested in the headset.

Visibility evidence:
0x50ba80 builds separate frusta from Renderer+4 and Renderer+0x58.
0x50bfb0 render_scene copies the first to camera 0x7c3114 / frustum 0x7c3168;
the second goes to rasterizer window parameters (camera 0x7c1228 / frustum
0x7c127c). 0x5537c0 performs BSP/portal visibility. Native 0x50cc40 uses the
camera's +0x44 clip plane when zNear at +0x3c is zero. R1.26 repositioned both
frusta but retained their inherited clip fields. There is no runtime trace
proving those fields caused the recorded holes. R1.27 therefore logs original
near/far values on entry and applies a conservative cockpit visibility fix:
- after current HMD/eye pose, use a regular positive near plane (1/256 world
  unit); retain a valid native far plane; reset the explicit clip plane;
- copy that eye to the culling frustum and add 20 degrees to the culling FOV,
  bounded at 150 degrees and never narrower than the headset FOV;
- leave raster FOV, eye position/facing/up, vehicle interpolation, yaw, physics
  and portal/PVS machinery unchanged. No global no-culling patch or new hook;
- extract overlays from the raster frustum, not the widened culling frustum;
- do nothing on foot, unsupported seats, or an unsuccessful camera override.
The margin may submit somewhat more geometry while seated. It is not a
measured performance result or a guarantee that the specific BSP issue is fixed.
