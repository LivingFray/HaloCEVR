Camera:
- Chimera applies render interpolation to object node matrices and restores them
  after rendering. It leaves ObjectDatum.position un-interpolated. The old
  camera used an interpolated head against a simulation vehicle basis plus its
  own frame-timestamp buffer. The two timelines can disagree with the cockpit.
- Read/validate the vehicle model's existing live node block (same validated
  node offset/allocation reader as bipeds). Convert root matrix by inverse bind
  to get the rendered chassis basis. Read the seated biped root for turret yaw.
- Use this basis for BOTH head-local entry capture and final seat position.
  Never add another interpolation delay when the rendered basis is available.
  HMD look/lean remains direct; no motion values are written into native objects.
- Three-second entry settling and custom-map simulation fallback are retained.
- Real engine/GPU phase alignment and comfort still require headset testing.
